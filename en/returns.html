Return & Exchange Policy

At Rawnaq, we aim to provide a smooth and fair shopping experience. If an issue occurs with your order, the following policy applies.

1) Return Window

Return requests are accepted within 7 days of delivery, provided the item remains in its original condition and unused.

2) Eligible Return Cases

  • The delivered item is different from what was ordered.
  • The package arrived visibly damaged (supported by clear photos).
  • The product arrived defective due to shipping handling.

3) Non-eligible Cases

  • The product has been opened or used in a way that affects resale condition.
  • The return request is submitted after the allowed period.
  • Personal scent preference after use.

4) Return Process

  1. Contact customer support through the Contact page.
  2. Share your order number and relevant product photos if needed.
  3. The case is reviewed within 1-2 business days.

5) Refund or Replacement

Once approved, the order may be replaced or refunded through the original payment method according to the payment provider processing timeline.

6) Shipping Fees

Shipping charges are covered by the store when the fault is on our side. In other accepted cases, return shipping fees may apply to the customer.
